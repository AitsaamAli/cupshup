# Kitchen Display Guide — Cup Shup POS

## What you'll see

Every order sent from the counter shows up here within a second —
nothing to refresh, nothing to check manually. Each ticket shows the
table/order number, how long it's been waiting (colour-coded: grey is
fine, amber means it's getting slow, red means it's late), and only
**your station's** items — Hot Kitchen doesn't see the chai order, Chai
station doesn't see the burger.

## Working a ticket

- Tap an item to move it forward: **pending → preparing → ready**.
- Tap **"All ready"** once every item on your side of the ticket is
  done — that's what tells the counter/waiter the table is ready.
- If a ticket got bumped by mistake, tap **Recall** to bring it back.

## Running out of something

Tap the **86** icon next to an item right from the ticket. It
disappears from the counter's ordering screen immediately — no need to
walk over and tell the cashier.

## Printing a paper ticket

Tap the print icon on a ticket if you want a paper copy for your
station (in addition to the screen — this is a backup, not required).

## This screen never times out

Unlike the counter screen, KDS stays logged in for the whole shift —
you won't get logged out for being idle. No PIN re-entry needed while
you're cooking.
