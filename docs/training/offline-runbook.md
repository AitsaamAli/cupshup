# Internet Band Ho To Kya Karein — Cup Shup POS

## Keep working. The register does not stop.

You'll see an **"Offline"** badge appear near the top of the screen the
moment the connection drops. That's the only thing that changes for
you:

- Keep taking orders exactly as normal. Each one is saved on this
  device immediately and will send itself the moment the connection is
  back — you don't need to do anything to make that happen.
- The badge shows how many orders are waiting to send, e.g.
  **"Offline — 3 orders pending."**

## What still works offline

- Taking new orders (dine-in, takeaway, delivery)
- The menu, including prices — showing whatever was last synced
- Printing a kitchen ticket and a customer receipt (local printer only)

## What does NOT work offline

- **Card / JazzCash / EasyPaisa payments** — these need a live
  connection to authorise. Take cash, or hold the payment until the
  connection is back.
- **The PRA fiscal number and QR code on the receipt** — the receipt
  still prints, with your shop's own invoice number, but the government
  QR code shows "PRA verification pending" until it syncs. This is
  expected and not an error.
- **Adding more items to a table's order that was already sent** — if a
  table wants a second round while you're offline, start a **new**
  order for it rather than adding to the open one; it'll appear as two
  tickets instead of one, which is fine.
- **Live stock counts** — 86'd items and price changes made on another
  terminal while you're offline won't show up until reconnected.

## When the connection comes back

The queued orders send themselves — watch the badge count go to zero.
If anything couldn't send (a rare case — for example, the day genuinely
closed while you were offline), you'll see a red **"couldn't sync"**
badge instead. Tell a manager; nothing was lost, but that one needs a
human decision, not an automatic retry.
